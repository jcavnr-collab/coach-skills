# Morning inbox triage

Use this prompt only after the coach chooses the account scope, local time zone, schedule and lookback period. Creating a schedule requires the coach's request. Connect CinchOS Coaching and Inbox as that coach.

---

Follow the inbox-triage skill for my confirmed connected mailboxes.

1. Use my writing-style profile if available, or my saved writing preferences.
2. Read unread mail within the agreed time window, opening threads only as needed.
3. Group messages into Athletes, Prospects, Partners and business, and Noise.
4. Draft replies in the conversation with their message references. Do not claim that they were saved to Gmail.
5. Do not send, archive, mark read, create email drafts, or change any records.
6. Summarize Needs you today, Replies drafted here, and Suggested archiving. Flag money, injury, complaints and time-sensitive decisions for me.

Keep email content inside the run. Use the current coach's authorized mailboxes, never an example account or another coach's identity.
