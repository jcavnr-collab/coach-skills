# Default process style

View ../assets/sample-process.png and inspect its editable companion. The example is fictional and contains no account information.

Use a white canvas, concise labels and enough space to follow connectors. Prefer an action rectangle, a decision diamond, and an ellipse for a beginning or result. Use headings to identify owners or phases rather than enclosing every note.

Default text uses Excalidraw fontFamily 3. Use roughness 0, solid fills and full opacity. Larger titles and smaller notes should remain readable at the intended export size.

Use dark text on light fills and white text on dark fills. Add short branch labels near their connectors. Keep crossings away from labels, and reserve room for revision loops. The coach's explicit style or brand requirements take precedence.
